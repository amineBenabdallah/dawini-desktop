3 Response Trust Decision

'Dorosz p.34' or
'CIM-10' or 'MSPRH'"] CITE -->|No| BADGE_WARN["Warning badge:

Key principle: Amira never says "I think" or "In my opinion" — she always says "D'après [source]" or "Je n'ai pas cette information." The trust is in the documents, not in the AI.
